PlanWise AI

Generative AI event planner

Plan unforgettable events — in minutes, not months.

Tell PlanWise about your wedding, birthday or community event. Generative AI builds a complete plan: a realistic budget, a dated timeline, a vendor checklist and personalized ideas — then stays with you as a chat assistant.

Bring your own AI key (Gemini, OpenAI, Groq…) — keys stay in your browser. No key? The built-in Smart Planner still generates a complete plan.

Smart budgets

AI allocates your budget across categories with per-guest guidance and a contingency buffer, visualized in a live chart.

Dated timelines

Milestones are computed backward from your event date with checkable tasks and progress tracking.

Vendor checklists

Prioritized vendors with cost estimates and the exact questions to ask before you sign anything.

Personalized ideas

Themes, décor, menus, activities and favors tailored to your vibe — regenerate any category on demand.

AI assistant chat

Ask anything about your plan: draft invitations, vendor emails, toasts or a rain-plan — with full plan context.

Save & export

Plans are saved in your browser. Export as JSON or Markdown, or print a clean PDF-ready document.
